# 🇩🇪 Augsburg - Dinkelsbühl
This scenic bike tour leads from the historic city of Augsburg along the Romantic Road to the medieval gem of Dinkelsbühl. The route offers a mix of beautiful landscapes, charming old towns, and cultural highlights.

## Day 1: Augsburg - Nördlingen
A standout along the way is Harburg, home to one of Germany’s oldest and best-preserved castles, dramatically perched above the Wörnitz River. With its fully intact city wall and historic old town, Nördlingen is the perfect place for an overnight stay, inviting you to explore its fascinating history and relaxed atmosphere.

You can skip the detour to the Donaumeile in Donauwörth, if you don't need to buy something.

### Food and accommodation
Food options along the route are sparse and mostly closed (for good?). Try to fill your belly in the larger cities. Family-run *Wie bei Oma*[^1] offers good german-croatian food in the center of Donauwörth. The *Brückencafè*[^2] in Harburg is a good option for coffee and cake with a view. *Blaue Glocke*[^3] in Nördlingen provides a cozy vibe together with delicious Swabian food for reasonable prices - Recommendation!

*Hey Lou Hotel Nördlingen*[^4] offers modern rooms and good value for the price. Bicycle storage: In a covered and locked "bicycle cage".

## Day 2: Nördlingen - Dinkelsbühl
Leaving Nördlingen through the Baldinger Tor, the route continues through villages and the countryside to Dinkelsbühl.

### Food
*Mayer's Beck Bäckerei*[^5] offers good coffee and breakfast menus. The restaurant *Zur Glocke*[^6] serves tasty German and Franconian classics at the central square of Dinkelsbühl. If you have some time until the next train in Wassertrüdingen, you can have a Spalter beer at the *Spalter Bierstube*[^7]. In case you miss your connecting train in Gunzenhausen, the *Lighthouse Restaurant*[^8] near the station offers good food and service.
