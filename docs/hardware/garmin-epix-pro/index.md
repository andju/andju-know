# Garmin Epix Pro
When I looked for a digital sports watch, one thing was important for me: I don't want to upload (sensitive) health and activity data into a cloud. I therefore came across Garmin watches: They combine good hardware quality with the ability to access their data via USB. Almost all information is stored in the [.fit format](https://developer.garmin.com/fit/overview/).

Most articles on this page are about managing the watch without synchronizing with Garmin Connect.
