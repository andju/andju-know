# Prague
The "Golden City" became one of the most important cities under Charles IV. Today it is one of the most beautiful European cities.

## Sights
There are plenty of sights as well as guide books, blogs and websites describing them.

## Food and accommodation
Even though there are plenty of eating places in the city, there are many tourist traps (multi-language menus are a bad sign). You can head out to the neighborhood of Žižkov which is less-touristy.

### [Bicycle tour (2022): 🇨🇿 Kingdom Come Deliverance](cz-kingdom-come-deliverance.md)
A good restaurant in the city is *Restaurace Pod Petřínem*[^1]. In the neighborhood of Žižkov you will find *U Sadu*[^2] which serves good food and its own beer.

The *Exe City Park*[^3] hotel is conveniently located across the street from the main station, offers nice rooms and good breakfast. Bicycle storage: In an empty (locked) conference room.

### [Bicycle tour (2025): 🇵🇱 South Lower Silesia ](pl-south-lower-silesia.md)
*[Café Imperial](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caf%C3%A9_Imperial_(Prag))*[^4] offers high class food in an Art Nouveau surrounding, while *Restaurace U Bansethů*[^5] has classic Czech food for good value.

[^3]: Exe City Park, Opletalova 960/33, 110 00 Nové Město
